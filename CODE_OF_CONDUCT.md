# Community conduct

Carbon Club is a shared waiting room beside focused work. Treat other people as people: no harassment, threats, impersonation, doxxing, sexual exploitation, spam, targeted hate or attempts to evade another participant's local block.

Public-beta operators may disconnect peers or close an invitation cohort to protect participants. Signed evidence can support review, but it must be handled with minimum necessary exposure and must not be reposted as entertainment.

The low-age room concept is not open during this beta. It requires independent child-safety, legal and moderation review before implementation.
